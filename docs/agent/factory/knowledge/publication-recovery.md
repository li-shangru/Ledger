The write-exclusion boundary spans drift/open-import checks through mutation. Pinned identities, owner-only modes, pre-mutation backups, durable manifests and idempotent recovery are distinct obligations. Proposal filing stores intent; preview/application retain the exact reviewed revisions and product approval boundary. Development authorization cannot supply that approval.

A successful in-memory or compact JSON protocol test does not prove native SQLite/PDF durability.

Relevant failure modes include time-of-check/time-of-use drift, inode/link substitution, contradictory state markers, incomplete durability, duplicate audit/publication and overlapping staging/mutation.
